### 🚀 About Me

Hey, I'm Harsh!  
I'm a Data Science postgraduate who likes exploring pretty much everything tech-related. From machine learning and data analysis to web development, Linux, networking, automation and messing around with Android.  
I enjoy building things, breaking them, figuring out why they broke, and occasionally getting them to work.  
Most of my repositories are experiments, projects I've built while learning, or ideas I wanted to turn into something practical.  
Always curious, always tinkering.

🔭 &nbsp;I'm currently working on **A mix of AI/ML projects, developer tools and random experiments**  
🌱 &nbsp;I'm currently learning **MLOps, LLMs, system design and better software development practices**  
👯 &nbsp;I'm looking to collaborate on **Interesting open-source projects, AI tools and anything tech-related**  
😄 &nbsp;Pronouns: **He/Him**  
⚡ &nbsp;Fun fact: **I have a habit of going down random tech rabbit holes and turning them into projects**
